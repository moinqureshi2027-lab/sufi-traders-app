# Sufi Traders — Online Shop

An online shop for **Sufi Traders, Tower Market, Hyderabad, Sindh** — balloons, party items, kids' toys, clay, painting kits, snacks and biscuits.

Customers browse 22 real products with photos, add them to a cart, and order in two ways:

1. **WhatsApp Order** — the order is sent straight to the shop's WhatsApp number **03173016695** (Cash on Delivery; payment can also be discussed on WhatsApp).
2. **Online Payment** — JazzCash or Easypaisa. The shop number **03173016695** is copied automatically so the customer can paste it into their JazzCash/Easypaisa app, pay, and upload the payment screenshot in the app. The seller checks the payment herself.

## Delivery charges

- Hyderabad City: **Rs 200**
- Out of City: **Rs 350**

The checkout shows the full breakdown: products total + delivery = grand total.

## For the seller

- **Orders** — every order placed on the shop, with customer name, mobile, items, totals, delivery area, payment method and status. Tap **Confirm** and a confirmation message opens on WhatsApp to the customer's own number.
- **Add Product** — add a new product with name, price, category and photo from the phone.

## Features

- 22 products with real photos and Roman Urdu descriptions
- Categories: Balloons, Kids' Toys, Snacks — plus search
- 1 pc / Full pack options (sunglasses, party popper, mini torch) and per-kg pricing (biscuits, Top Pops)
- Mobile-first design: big buttons, sticky cart bar, easy one-thumb use
- Products without a price yet show "Price: WhatsApp par poochhein"
